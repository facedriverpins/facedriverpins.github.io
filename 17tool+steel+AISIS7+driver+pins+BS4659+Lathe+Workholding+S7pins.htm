S7 is a shock‑resisting tool steel defined by ASTM A681 (AISI S7, UNS T41907). Its international equivalents include 1.2355 (DIN), 50CrMoV13‑15 (EN/ISO), and 5Cr3Mn1SiMo1V (GB/T 1299 – China). The chemistry is carefully balanced: 0.45–0.55% C, 3.00–3.50% Cr, 1.30–1.80% Mo, ≤0.35% V, with Si, Mn, and the balance iron. This composition gives S7 two critical advantages – extreme impact toughness and excellent hardenability.

S7 operates at a working hardness of 54–58 HRC, with the sweet spot at 54–56 HRC for maximum fracture resistance. Its Charpy V‑notch values far exceed those of cold‑work grades. In short: S7 delivers the toughness you need when the cut is unpredictable.

S7 pins work alongside the face driver’s center point – the point provides precise axial location, while the S7 pins deliver the torque. Together they enable one‑chance setups that replace chucks and centers, boosting productivity by 200% or more.

We produce any quantity – from a prototype set of three pins to production batches of thousands. Every pin is backed by our deep knowledge of tool steel metallurgy and end‑face driving.
