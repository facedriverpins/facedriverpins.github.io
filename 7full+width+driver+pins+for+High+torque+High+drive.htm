In face driving systems, not all drive pins are created equal. Some are designed for small diameters, others for offset applications. But when your goal is maximum torque transmission and aggressive metal removal, only one configuration delivers: Full Width drive pins.

A Full Width drive pin is the standard – and strongest – layout in end‑face driving. Unlike lowered pins that sit close to the center axis, or offset pins that reach far outward, Full Width pins are distributed evenly across the driver’s nominal working diameter range. This symmetric arrangement maximizes the contact area between the pins and the workpiece end face, providing the largest possible grip and the highest driving force.

Full Width pins are the go‑to choice for roughing and semi‑finishing operations where heavy cuts, high feed rates, and interrupted cuts are common. They routinely handle driving forces of 3,000 to 7,800 pounds depending on driver size, making them ideal for large shafts, gear blanks, and automotive transmission components.
