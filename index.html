We are a manufacturer of drive pins for face drivers on turning, milling, drilling, grinding, and CNC lathes, producing high‑precision, wear‑resistant drive pins that ensure optimal torque transmission, reliable workpiece centering, and extended tool life for both standard and special face driving applications. What truly sets us apart is our exclusive focus on customizing these consumable drive pins for our customers’ unique face driver systems.

For over ten years, we have worked side by side with machine shops, production lines, and OEMs to deliver drive pins that are not generic replacements but precision‑engineered components tailored to specific workpiece materials, hardness levels, surface conditions, and machining parameters. Whether you are turning hardened steel, milling aluminum, drilling deep holes, or grinding shafts, we can adjust the pin geometry, tip angle, carbide grade, and surface treatment to optimize bite force, minimize end‑face damage, and maximize service life. Our engineering team understands that every face driver model from different brands has its own pin pocket design, spring preload, and balancing requirements, so we reverse‑engineer, dimensionally inspect, and dynamically match each custom pin set to your exact fixture.
