manufacturer of Precision Drive Pins for face drivers . supplier of face driver drive pins on turning, milling.

In the world of face drivers and end-face drive systems, the drive pin is far more than a simple component—it is the very point where torque meets the workpiece. As a dedicated manufacturer of high-performance drive pins, we understand that no two machining applications are identical. Whether you are hard turning gear shafts, grinding camshafts, or milling splines on a robotic actuator, the geometry, hardness, and arrangement of drive pins directly determine clamping force, transmission accuracy, and process stability. That is why we have built our entire production philosophy around one principle: customization without compromise.

We do not supply off-the-shelf pins and hope they fit your process. Instead, we start with your specific requirements. You may have a detailed engineering drawing—perhaps with unusual flank angles, a non-standard tip radius, or a specific carbide grade for materials above 65 HRC. We machine every feature to your print, using precision grinding and EDM where needed, with tolerances down to ±0.005 mm. No drawing is too complex, no batch size too small.

Why is this custom approach so critical? Because drive pins operate at the extreme edge of mechanics. A standard pin may leave insufficient penetration in soft alloys, causing slip, or too aggressive an indentation in hardened steel, leading to cracking. By tailoring the tip angle, the number of cutting edges, and the carbide substrate (e.g., micro-grain vs. ultra-fine), we ensure that each pin grips exactly as required—neither too light nor too deep. This extends tool life, protects your spindle bearings, and produces consistent part quality.
