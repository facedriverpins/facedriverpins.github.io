Contact us today with your drawing, sample, or driver model. We deliver toughness where it matters most! In high-end face driving systems, standard drive pins work well for routine cylindrical parts. But what happens when the workpiece is eccentric? When the end face is uneven? When the cut is heavily interrupted? That is where offset driver pins become indispensable. And when those offset pins must survive brutal impact without shattering, only S7 tool steel delivers the necessary toughness.

At our facility, we specialize in exactly this intersection: custom S7 offset driver pins, made precisely to your drawing, your sample, or your face driver model number.

Unlike conventional pins arranged symmetrically around the driver’s axis, offset pins are deliberately positioned at a non‑center radial distance. “Offset” can mean two things: a geometrically shifted mounting location that changes the effective driving diameter, or a dedicated pin style—often called a half‑offset pin—that extends the driver’s working range. More importantly, offset pins allow each pin to engage the workpiece independently, compensating for end‑face irregularities, tapers up to 5–7 degrees, or parts with pre‑existing bores or shoulders. They are the solution when a standard pin layout simply cannot reach or grip.
